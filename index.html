How to add your team

iPhone: tap iPhone next to your team and confirm.

Android: tap Android, it opens Google Calendar and asks you to confirm. If it refuses on mobile, do it once in a desktop browser and it syncs to your phone.

These are subscriptions, not one-off imports. This means that if a match gets rearranged or cancelled, your calendar corrects itself. Refresh isn't instant (Google can sometimes take up to a day) so always check the group chat for last-minute changes.
